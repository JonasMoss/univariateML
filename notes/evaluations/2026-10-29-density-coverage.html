extraDistr and actuar: MLE coverage

Audit date: 29 October 2026. Inventory comes from installed namespace exports; coverage comes from distribution folders in this checkout, including recently added families whose board tasks still say queued. This is an API and model-domain audit, not a new numerical validation of the fitters or reference d/p/q/r functions.

Covered means a fitter exists within its documented sample, fixed-design and boundary policies. Equivalent means a reparameterization or alias, not an exported wrapper for that name. Partial / restricted is deliberately not counted as full coverage. Rejecting a model is a proposed policy here: no error-only fitters were added.

Inclusion decisions and accepted study candidates are recorded in the density scope decisions. Bhattacharjee and categorical are deliberately excluded, along with multivariate laws.

What is worth adding?

The most natural next projects are Skellam, discrete Laplace, discrete normal, discrete gamma, zero-endpoint half-normal and the half-Cauchy/half-t pair. These are missing fitter candidates, not claims that every sample has an attained MLE. Arbitrary fixed truncation of Poisson/binomial is also a useful extension. Categorical is excluded because its unrestricted probability vector has a dimension determined by the category set; Bhattacharjee is excluded as too specialized. Negative hypergeometric needs design decisions. Slash needs its own boundary analysis. Poisson mixtures and phase-type fitting are larger projects.

Where an explicit error makes sense

For a freely translated Weibull, fix any shape k between 0 and 1 and any positive scale. Let mu approach the sample minimum from below. The density at each minimum is proportional to (min(x)-mu)k-1 and diverges, while other densities approach finite positive values. Thus the likelihood is unbounded. A known threshold still gives the ordinary two-parameter Weibull after subtraction. Shifted Weibull is not itself a density export in either audited package.

The same endpoint argument rejects freely bounded nonstandard beta (shape1<1 at a lower endpoint or shape2<1 at an upper endpoint), generalized beta with free scale and shape2<1, and freely translated Pareto III/IV and Feller–Pareto. Keep all other parameters fixed along each path. These conclusions follow directly from their documented density powers. They do not rule out fixed-endpoint or shape-constrained models. The existing fatigue README gives its separate free-location collapse argument. Normal mixtures have a component-collapse path.

Free-location Pareto II also has unbounded likelihood: with k minima among n observations, set min=m-epsilon and scale=epsilon. The likelihood is proportional to epsilonalpha(n-k)-k, which diverges for sufficiently small positive alpha. This corrects the earlier classification as partial coverage.

Suggested diagnostic: “Unrestricted maximum likelihood is unbounded when the endpoint is estimated. Supply a fixed endpoint or use a documented constrained model.” Only use this after identifying the actual free-parameter model. Do not give this error merely because an otherwise valid family's MLE fails for a particular sample.

Counting traps

actuar's log-Gompertz is inverse Weibull; Pearson6 is its transformed-beta alias; Poisson-inverse-Gaussian has two names. Its generalized Pareto is beta-prime, whereas extraDistr's generalized Pareto is the threshold/EVT family. extraDistr's gamma-Poisson is negative binomial. A phase-type observation is scalar, so matrix-valued parameters do not make that law multivariate. Fixed half-normal is not covered exactly by fitting an additional translation. Full free-location Fréchet exceeds the present GEV domain.
