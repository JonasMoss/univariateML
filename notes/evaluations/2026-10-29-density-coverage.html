extraDistr and actuar: MLE coverage

Audit date: 29 October 2026. Inventory comes from installed namespace exports; coverage comes from distribution folders in this checkout, including recently added families whose board tasks still say queued. This is an API and model-domain audit, not a new numerical validation of the fitters or reference d/p/q/r functions.

Covered means a fitter exists within its documented sample, fixed-design and boundary policies. Covered (endpoint held): the unrestricted likelihood is unbounded for every sample, so the fitter requires the endpoint and its stop message says why. Equivalent means a reparameterization or alias, not an exported wrapper for that name. Partial / restricted is deliberately not counted as full coverage.

Inclusion decisions are recorded in the density scope decisions (full coverage plan, 29 October 2026). extraDistr's Tukey lambda exports only qtlambda/rtlambda, so it is not a row below; it is covered by mltlambda with the package's own d/p/q/r, lambda in [-1, 1].

Remaining

The vector-parameter tier (categorical, Poisson and normal mixtures) needs parameter vectors whose length the user chooses. The partial rows keep documented restrictions: fatigue's location is fixed at zero, the GEV and GPD shapes are bounded, and the location-scale t has df at least one.

Counting traps

actuar's log-Gompertz is inverse Weibull; Pearson6 is its transformed-beta alias; Poisson-inverse-Gaussian has two names. Its generalized Pareto is beta-prime, whereas extraDistr's generalized Pareto is the threshold/EVT family. extraDistr's gamma-Poisson is negative binomial. A phase-type observation is scalar, so matrix-valued parameters do not make that law multivariate.
