# P0: extreme-value families, holds and MLE existence

2026-10-29; updated on continuation. **Incomplete:** the tables cover all
21 hold subsets, but the face-comparison criteria marked † rely on an
unproved exhaustion of all noncompact paths, and no globally certified
root enumeration was implemented. Numerical multistart evidence is not a
proof of those criteria. The literal `extraDistr` endpoint convention is
material; the design's claimed attained `xi=-1` boundary is false under it.

## Conventions and how to read the tables

All samples are finite; `m=min(x)`, `M=max(x)`, `k=# {x_i=m}`,
`d_i=x_i-m`, and `n` is the sample size. Unless stated otherwise samples
are nonconstant. Holds must be finite, have positive scale and satisfy the
strict support; empty feasible sets have no likelihood. GPD requires
`mu<m`, Fréchet requires `mu<m`. “Never” means no finite MLE for the
specified case, not that no probability law exists. `xi=-1` is a uniform
GPD or reversed-exponential GEV **as a probability law**; a density at
an individual endpoint can be chosen in multiple ways. Here the likelihood
uses the actual reference version, which is **zero** at either endpoint.
The results would differ if the package adopted a closed-endpoint version.

`C` below means the highest likelihood among admissible **finite**
candidates: interior stationary points and attained parameter-boundary
stationary points, including all roots, not just the first root found.
If no candidate exists, take `C=-Inf`. Comparison with a nonattained face
`B` is `C >= B`; equality still permits a finite MLE if a candidate attains
it. Do not use a numerical optimizer's failure to improve on B as proof.
The tables identify the unrestricted pathology and then classify the
intended R5-restricted problem where meaningful. For positive fixed xi,
write `q=k/(n-k)`; for xi=0 use its limiting density.

Status describes the master API: **yes** accepts that held set; **partial**
accepts only the stated shapes/data and/or treats missing `mu` as a hold;
**no** does not estimate the free coefficients for that set. The numerical
comparison is below, not an assertion of correctness outside the exercised
cases.

## GEV

Let `B_gev = -n log(s)-sum((M-x)/s)`. Use `s=mean(M-x)` with
neither mu nor sigma held; `s=sigma` with sigma held; `s=M-mu` with
mu held and `M>mu`. This is the reversed-exponential face at xi=-1,
endpoint M, excluded by the reference. For `mu,sigma` held, this face
exists only if `M-mu=sigma`. Let `A_free=n log 2-n-2 sum_{x_i>m} log d_i`
and `A_mu=-k-2 sum_{x_i>m} log d_i`; these are the xi=1 collapse faces
when `k=n/2`, respectively with free mu and with mu held at m.

| Held | Class, conditions, limit or mechanism | Master |
|---|---|---|
| none | Unrestricted **never**: xi<-1 endpoint or xi>1 collapse. Restricted `[-1,1]`: if k>n/2, **never**, collapse; if k<=n/2, **data-dependent** †: finite candidate wins iff `C>=B_gev` and, at equality of multiplicities, `C>=A_free`. Otherwise reversed exponential or collapse face. | partial: xi in [-.5,1], n>=3, every tie < n/2; successes agree in checks |
| mu | Unrestricted **never** for all positions: if mu<m, xi<-1 endpoint; if mu>=m, positive-shape collapse or large-xi spike (below). Restricted: **data-dependent** † when `M>mu`, comparing `C` to `B_gev`; if `mu=m`, additionally k>n/2 diverges and k=n/2 requires comparison with `A_mu`. When `mu>=M` and the sample is nonconstant there is no upper-endpoint face and no displayed collapse; attainment is conjectured. | no |
| sigma | Unrestricted **never**, large-xi spike even with scale fixed. Restricted: **data-dependent** †: compare `C` with `B_gev`; otherwise reversed-exponential endpoint. | no |

For -1<xi<=0, **always** for nonconstant data. | partial: only [-.5,1], n>=3, every tie < n/2; successes agree |

When `M<=mu` or `sigma<M-mu`, **always** for nonconstant data; at `sigma=M-mu`, **data-dependent** by comparison with `B_gev`. Restricted: **always** except `M-mu=sigma`, where **data-dependent** † by comparison of `C` and `B_gev`. | no |

If xi>0 and mu=m: **never** for `xi*q>=1`, divergent if strict and finite collapse face if equal; **always** for `xi*q<1`. For xi>-1 and mu!=m, **always** for nonconstant data. For xi<-1 and M<=mu, **always** for nonconstant data. | no |
| sigma,xi | **Never** for xi<-1 (endpoint) and xi=-1 (reversed-exponential face); **always** for xi>-1, even with ties or n=1. | no |

At fixed xi>0 with mu and sigma free, the equality face is finite and
unattained: optimizing the minimum-density term fixes its standardized
coordinate, and every nonminimum density is strictly below its limiting
power-law envelope. The same argument with mu=m proves the corresponding
fixed-mu equality. A tie away from the minimum does not create the positive
shape collapse. Master rejects e.g. a tie of half the observations at the
*maximum* even when xi=0, for which a nonconstant Gumbel sample has an MLE.

## GPD

Put `y=x-mu`, `Y=max(y)` for held mu. `B_gpd=-n log Y` is the
uniform upper-endpoint face. For sigma held and mu free define
`F(xi) = -n log(sigma) -(1+1/xi) sum log(1+xi*(x-m)/sigma)`, using its
continuous value at zero, on `xi>-1`, `xi<=1`, and positive support.
It is the likelihood as mu approaches m, and is not itself attained.

| Held | Class, conditions, limit or mechanism | Master |
|---|---|---|
| none | **Never**, even with -1<=xi<=1: threshold tends to m; at xi=-1 the interval must shrink to `(m,M)`; unrestricted also has collapse/upper-endpoint divergence. | no: missing/NULL mu is held at 0 |
| mu | Unrestricted **never**, endpoint xi<-1. With xi>=-1, **data-dependent**: compare all finite candidates with `B_gpd`; otherwise the uniform upper-endpoint face is unattained (Grimshaw criterion below). | partial: xi>=-.5 and x>mu; successes agree |
| sigma | Unrestricted **never**: endpoint divergence when `sigma>M-m`; otherwise the free-threshold face is excluded. With -1<=xi<=1, **data-dependent**: an MLE exists iff `sigma>M-m` and `sup F<=-n log(sigma)`; it is then any feasible xi=-1 uniform plateau in mu. Otherwise threshold→m, or the xi=-1 endpoint face, gives the supremum. | no |
| xi | **Never**: for xi>-1 increase mu toward m; xi=-1 shrinks the uniform interval; xi<-1 gives endpoint divergence. | no: xi accepted only while mu held |
| mu,sigma | Unrestricted: if `sigma>Y` endpoint shape `-sigma/Y` is below -1 and diverges; if `sigma<Y`, **always**; at equality, **data-dependent** by the uniform-face comparison. With xi>=-1, **always** when `sigma!=Y`; when `sigma=Y`, **data-dependent** by finite-candidate comparison with `B_gpd`. | no |
| mu,xi | For xi>-1 **always**, unique positive scale root for all y>0, including constant samples and n=1. xi=-1 **never**: `sigma↓Y` is the uniform face. xi<-1 **never**: endpoint divergence. | partial: xi>=-.5 only; successes agree |
| sigma,xi | xi>-1 **never**, mu→m; xi=-1 **always** when `sigma>M-m`, with plateau `M-sigma<mu<m`; xi<-1 **never**, endpoint divergence if `sigma/(-xi)>M-m`. Otherwise support is empty. | no |

For fixed mu and xi>-1, the scale score multiplied by sigma is
`(1+xi) mean(y/(sigma+xi*y))-1`, strictly decreasing from positive to
negative on the feasible scale interval; this proves the unique root.
For fixed sigma and free mu, the GPD log likelihood is strictly increasing
in mu for xi>-1, whereas xi=-1 gives a plateau. This proves the displayed
`sup F` condition without assuming profile concavity.

## Fréchet

For fixed lambda, put `p=lambda*(n-k)-k`. At p=0 the free-mu/free-sigma
collapse face is `A_lambda = n log(lambda*n/k)-n
-(lambda+1) sum_{x_i>m} log d_i`. Let `B_G` be the maximized Gumbel
log likelihood (`mlgumbel`), and `A_free` as in the GEV section.

| Held | Class, conditions, limit or mechanism | Master |
|---|---|---|
| none | Unrestricted **never**, collapse. With lambda>=1: k>n/2 **never**, collapse; k<=n/2 **data-dependent** †: `C>=B_G` and, if k=n/2, `C>=A_free`; otherwise Gumbel or collapse face. | partial: lambda>=1, every tie < n/2, numerical Gumbel comparison; successes agree |
| lambda | p<0 **never**, divergent collapse; p=0 **never**, finite unattained collapse; p>0 **always** for nonconstant data. | no |
| mu | **Always** if x>mu and sample nonconstant: inverse Weibull on x-mu (`mlinvweibull`, with rate `1/sigma`). Constant sample **never**, lambda→infinity with sigma=x-mu. | yes; successes agree |
| sigma | Unrestricted **never**, spike as lambda→0 and mu→m, even though scale is held. With lambda>=1, **always** for nonconstant data †; constant data **never**, lambda→infinity at mu=x-sigma. | no |
| lambda,mu | **Always**, including constant and n=1: `sigma^lambda=n/sum((x-mu)^(-lambda))`. | no |
| lambda,sigma | **Always**, including constant and n=1: likelihood is continuous in mu<m and vanishes as mu approaches either endpoint. | no |
| mu,sigma | **Always** except constant x-mu=sigma, where **never**, lambda→infinity. For constant x-mu!=sigma there is a finite maximum. | no |

With held mu and sigma, put `z_i=log((x_i-mu)/sigma)`. The log likelihood
in lambda is `n log lambda-lambda sum z_i-sum exp(-lambda z_i)+constant`.
It is strictly concave and tends to -Inf at both shape boundaries unless
all `z_i=0`. With held mu and free sigma, profiling sigma gives a strictly
monotone inverse-Weibull score for nonconstant data. At a fixed lambda with
free mu and sigma, the profiled likelihood has order `epsilon^p` as
mu=m-epsilon, and tends to zero as mu→-Inf. The equality case is not an
MLE: each nonminimum term lies strictly below its collapse envelope.

## Proofs and endpoint convention

For GPD or GEV fixed xi<-1, an upper endpoint approaching M gives a term
`(-1-1/xi) log(t)` with negative coefficient and `t→0+`, hence +Inf.
At xi=-1 this coefficient is zero. `extraDistr::dgpd(M,mu,M-mu,-1)`
and `dgev(M,mu,M-mu,-1)` both return zero, while their limits from inside
are positive. For GEV xi=-1 and endpoint b, density is
`exp(-(b-x)/sigma)/sigma`; maximizing in b takes b↓M. For GPD xi=-1
and fixed threshold, likelihood is sigma^-n for sigma>Y. Thus the
suprema named above are not reference-density MLEs.

For GPD `d log L/d mu=(1+xi) sum(1/(sigma+xi*(x-mu)))`. It is positive
for xi>-1. With xi=-1, jointly free mu and sigma shrink the uniform
interval to excluded `(m,M)`. Thus the M2 restriction `xi<=1` eliminates
some collapse paths but does not restore a free-threshold MLE. If xi>0,
the usual threshold/scale collapse has exponent `(n-k)/xi-k`; for tie
k<n/2, xi<=1 blocks it. This statement does not address the separate
threshold face.

For GEV with fixed sigma and free mu, take xi→infinity and
`mu=m+sigma/xi-sigma*exp(-a*xi)/xi`, a>0. The minimum contributes
`a*xi+O(1)` in log likelihood and other observations only `-log xi+O(1)`;
the likelihood diverges. With fixed mu>m and free sigma, let
`sigma=xi*(mu-m)/(1-exp(-a*xi))`: the same divergence occurs even though
sigma grows. With mu=m, the ordinary xi>1 scale collapse applies. If
mu<m, an xi<-1 endpoint can approach M. Thus holding location does not in
general remove the need for the upper shape restriction; for mu>m its
mechanism is a shape/endpoint spike, not the stated small-scale exponent.

For Fréchet with sigma held, choose `mu=m-sigma*exp(-L)` and
`lambda=log(c*L)/L`, 0<c<1. The minimum contributes
`(1-c)L+log(lambda)+O(1)`; every other observation contributes
`log(lambda)+O(1)`. Hence the likelihood diverges. This is a shape/endpoint
spike as lambda→0, distinct from the fixed-lambda, free-scale collapse.
Holding sigma does not justify dropping the lambda restriction when lambda
is free. Conversely, if lambda and sigma are both held, the free-mu MLE
exists even for lambda<1, so an unconditional lambda>=1 bound overrestricts.

## xi in [-1,-1/2), profile and literature

The `profile.R` experiment uses independent `extraDistr` log densities and
profiles over location and scale for GEV, scale for GPD with mu=0. Fixed
seeds; xi grids contain -1, -1+10^-j (j=2,...,8), and a .025 mesh to -.5;
truths -1, -.85, -.6, -.5, 0 and n=20,100,1000. It reports the *limiting*
xi=-1 value, not the reference density at the excluded endpoint. The
840 evaluations include profiles whose mesh maxima occur at the endpoint,
at interior shapes, or at -.5. For example, at n=100 and truth -.85,
the grid maxima are GEV -.8 and GPD -.85; at n=1000 and truth -1 both
have their grid maximum at -1. Profiles are not reliably represented by
one quadratic or a single root search.

With a positive number of maximum ties, profiling at xi=-1+delta yields a
negative `k_max*delta*log(1/delta)` contribution plus O(delta): an extremely
narrow boundary dip can be missed even by this grid. In the GPD truth -.85,
n=100 case the boundary is -14.84592233, the profile at delta=1e-7 is
-14.84592254, but at delta=.025 it is -14.61012. These are numerical
checks of particular profiles, not a proof of global shape or uniqueness.

Grimshaw (1993), *Technometrics* 35, 185–191,
[doi:10.1080/00401706.1993.10485040](https://doi.org/10.1080/00401706.1993.10485040),
pp. 187–190, profiles GPD and compares interior stationary candidates to
`-n log Y`; p. 189 explicitly says the open-set boundary is treated as
closed. That is the precise convention under which its boundary estimate
is attained, and is not the literal endpoint value of `extraDistr::dgpd`.
The paper also explains that the profile score has multiple roots, so its
root search must compare candidates. No claim here that a chosen finite
numerical mesh finds every root.

## Numerical evidence and remaining proof gap

All computations are fixed-seed and runnable from the repository root with
`/tmp/umvl-codex/simbox-run.sh Rscript notes/evaluations/2026-10-29-p0-extreme/<script>`.
`oracle.R` and `master-check.R` use multistart `optim` on the external
reference log density, never the package fitter, and separately call the
installed master fitter. `oracle-results.csv` covers all 7 subsets for 39
samples: simulate_default at n=20,100,1000; n=2,3,5; ties at and below
half, majority ties, constants, Gumbel, reverse exponential, exponential,
uniform, and inverse Weibull. `faces.R` adds held positions below, at,
inside and above the sample range and several held scales. `probes.R`
demonstrates divergent paths, finite unattained faces, and endpoint conventions.

Across 62 successful master fits in the hold sweep and 150 in the separate
fixed-shape/limit sweep, all 212 log likelihoods agree with the best found
oracle value within `1e-6*n`; maximum absolute difference/n is 2.92e-8.
The sweep also records rejected small-n, tie and constant samples. These
checks do **not** certify the global optimum; the optimizer uses finite
starts, can converge at near-boundary approximations, and applies master's
shape bounds for master comparisons. The oracle treats `xi=-1` only in the
separate profile, and `faces.csv` is descriptive, not a proof of its face
criterion. Fréchet/Gumbel on numerical ties is stopped by master within
`1e-8*n`, so exact equality requires separate treatment.

Still incomplete: rigorous proof that the indicated faces exhaust every
noncompact sequence in each † GEV and Fréchet hold case; globally
certified enumeration of candidate roots and hence actual classifications
for arbitrary supplied data; a formal coercivity write-up for the one-free-parameter GEV cases. More held
parameter positions and GEV/GPD `[-1,-.5)` examples would strengthen
numerical coverage, but do not replace these proofs. The literal endpoint
convention must be settled before implementing C2.

## Kinds, reference domains and draft YAML

Agree with GEV regular parameters, GPD mu `design`, Fréchet mu `threshold`.
The design/threshold distinction concerns data collection; mathematically
GPD mu is a threshold. README's GEV xi `[-.5,1]`, GPD xi `[-.5,Inf)`,
and Fréchet lambda `[1,Inf)` are narrower than the reference's accepted
xi real, xi real, lambda>0 respectively; fit restrictions belong in
`fit_domain`, not reference domains (R7).

`while_free` cannot express all disjunctions here: for example GEV's
upper-bound spike can occur with *either* mu or sigma held; Fréchet can
spike with sigma held only when lambda is free, while the ordinary
fixed-lambda collapse needs sigma free. The following proposed YAML is
therefore deliberately a schema **draft**, not a complete executable
specification. Bounds are shown in `domain`; `reason` must be made
conditional in the eventual schema and fixed-value violations must be
handled according to the tables.

```yaml
# gev
fit_domain:
  xi:
    domain: '[-1, 1]'
    restriction: {mechanism: collapse, while_free: [], reason: 'lower and upper applicability depend on which location/scale coefficients are free; see table'}
holds:
  - {held: [mu], status: not-implemented, reason: 'mu hold not implemented'}
  - {held: [sigma], status: not-implemented, reason: 'sigma hold not implemented'}
  - {held: [mu, sigma], status: not-implemented, reason: 'mu and sigma holds not implemented'}
  - {held: [mu, xi], status: not-implemented, reason: 'mu hold not implemented'}
  - {held: [sigma, xi], status: not-implemented, reason: 'sigma hold not implemented'}

# gpd
params:
  - {name: mu, kind: design}
fit_domain:
  xi:
    domain: '[-1, Inf)'
    restriction: {mechanism: endpoint, while_free: [], reason: 'xi below -1 can diverge when the upper endpoint can move to M'}
holds:
  - {held: [], status: no-mle, reason: 'free threshold approaches the excluded minimum'}
  - {held: [xi], status: no-mle, reason: 'free threshold or upper endpoint has no maximizer'}
  - {held: [sigma], status: not-implemented, reason: 'free threshold and held scale not implemented'}
  - {held: [mu, sigma], status: not-implemented, reason: 'sigma hold not implemented'}
  - {held: [sigma, xi], status: not-implemented, reason: 'free threshold and held scale not implemented'}

# frechet
params:
  - {name: mu, kind: threshold}
fit_domain:
  lambda:
    domain: '[1, Inf)'
    restriction: {mechanism: collapse, while_free: [mu], reason: 'free mu permits collapse with free sigma and a lambda-to-zero spike with fixed sigma; held lambda and sigma need no bound'}
holds:
  - {held: [lambda], status: not-implemented, reason: 'lambda hold not implemented'}
  - {held: [sigma], status: not-implemented, reason: 'sigma hold not implemented'}
  - {held: [lambda, mu], status: not-implemented, reason: 'lambda hold not implemented'}
  - {held: [lambda, sigma], status: not-implemented, reason: 'lambda and sigma holds not implemented'}
  - {held: [mu, sigma], status: not-implemented, reason: 'sigma hold not implemented'}
```

`no-mle` entries above assume the current strict support. If C2 adopts a
closed uniform endpoint, the GPD free-threshold entries and some face
comparisons change. The proposed restrictions are intended to block the
stated divergences, not a proof of existence for every held combination.
