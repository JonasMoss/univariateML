# Sichel: Poisson–generalized-inverse-Gaussian counts

**Status:** banked idea, 2026-11-11 (Jonas: "sounds cool enough"). Not
scheduled; no implementation authorized by this entry. Best done after
continuous GIG ([useful density proposals](2026-10-31-useful-density-proposals.md#gig--ml-high-dpqr-high)),
which needs the same Bessel seam and face analysis.

## Idea

Mix a Poisson count over a GIG mean,
$f(y)\propto y^{\nu-1}\exp\{-(\psi y+\chi/y)/2\}$. The pmf is

$$
p_k=\Bigl(\frac{\psi}{\psi+2}\Bigr)^{\nu/2}
    \Bigl(\frac{\chi}{\psi+2}\Bigr)^{k/2}
    \frac{K_{k+\nu}\bigl(\sqrt{\chi(\psi+2)}\bigr)}
         {k!\,K_\nu\bigl(\sqrt{\chi\psi}\bigr)} .
$$

Three coefficients, fixed length, so in scope. It nests or bounds the
overdispersed count families we already have:

| slice or face | mixing law | count family |
|---|---|---|
| $\nu=-1/2$, $\chi=\lambda$, $\psi=\lambda/\mu^2$ | inverse Gaussian | `pig` (interior slice) |
| $\chi\to0$, $\nu>0$ | gamma(shape $\nu$, rate $\psi/2$) | `nbinom`, size $\nu$, prob $\psi/(\psi+2)$ |
| $\psi\to0$, $\nu<0$ | inverse gamma | Poisson–inverse-gamma (power tail $k^{\nu-1}$), not in the package |
| degenerate mixing | point mass | `pois` |

Checked 2026-11-11 with mpmath at 30 digits (high-precision comparison,
not proof): the pmf at $\nu=-1/2$ matches the IG mixture by quadrature
($7\times10^{-30}$, $\mu=2.3$, $\lambda=0.7$, $k\le7$); at $\chi=10^{-20}$
it matches NB ($10^{-21}$, $\nu=1.7$, $\psi=0.9$); and masses sum to one
($\nu=-1.3$, $\chi=3.1$, $\psi=0.4$). The inverse-gamma face is unchecked.

## Why it is interesting here

- A three-parameter count model whose boundary contains NB, Poisson and a
  power-tailed Poisson–inverse-gamma: several competing faces, and
  $\nu=\pm\infty$ limits besides. Good material for the
  [ML-difficulties paper](2026-11-02-ml-difficulties-paper.md).
- $\nu$ is known to be weakly identified (flat profile over wide ranges).
  An honest "flat but attained" versus "escapes to a face" classification is
  exactly the library's claim.
- Real users: species abundance, word frequencies and bibliometrics
  (Sichel's own applications), crash counts and proteomics spectral counts
  (see the PIG-versus-Sichel link in the
  [aliases report](../research/evaluations/2026-10-31-aliases-report.md)).
  `gamlss.dist::SICHEL`/`SI` is the common implementation.

## Open questions

1. Does PIG's exact mean reduction ($\hat\mu=\bar x$ with the other
   coordinate held in a suitable chart) survive for general $\nu$? Unchecked.
2. Can the PIG compact-coordinate concavity proof (Bessel-polynomial
   variance bound) extend to non-half-integer orders, for example through
   the Riccati equation for $z K_{\mu+1}/K_\mu$ used in
   [the PIG analysis](../research/evaluations/2026-10-29-pig/analysis.md)?
3. Existence: which samples send the fit to NB, Poisson–inverse-gamma or
   Poisson, and is equality/tie behaviour as clean as PIG's
   variance-versus-mean criterion?
4. Parameterization for users: gamlss $(\mu,\sigma,\nu)$, the
   Stein–Zucchini–Juritz chart, or GIG $(\nu,\chi,\psi)$. Mean as a role is
   needed for model selection against `nbinom` and `pig`.
5. Whether Poisson–inverse-gamma deserves its own family as the
   $\psi\to0$ face.

## d/p/q/r sketch

General order removes the Bessel-polynomial trick used by `pig`. Compute
$\log K_\nu$ and $\log K_{\nu+1}$ once at $z=\sqrt{\chi(\psi+2)}$ and run the
forward recurrence $K_{\mu+1}=K_{\mu-1}+(2\mu/z)K_\mu$, stable for $K$, as
a positive ratio recurrence in log space (as `pig_detail::log_masses` does).
The normalizer $K_\nu(\sqrt{\chi\psi})$ at small argument (near the NB face)
and large $\lvert\nu\rvert$ needs log-scale evaluation; `special.hpp` has no
Bessel wrapper yet, and Boost's range must be assessed. Tails: the ratio
tends to $2/(\psi+2)$, so the PIG/NB geometric-remainder bounds should carry
over for $\psi>0$; the $\psi=0$ face has a power tail and needs its own.

## Verification

- mpmath truth table using `besselk` at arbitrary order; check the
  $\nu=-1/2$ slice against `pig` truth and the $\chi\to0$ limit against
  `nbinom`.

- Fits: multistart oracle plus face likelihoods on samples built near each
  face; goldens after the existence study.
