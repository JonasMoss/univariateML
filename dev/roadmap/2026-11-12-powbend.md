# Power-bend: discrete power law with exponential cutoff

**Status:** banked, 2026-11-12 (Jonas: "lets do that"), from the
[sads survey](../research/evaluations/2026-11-12-sads-survey.md). Not
scheduled; no implementation authorized by this entry. Priority above the
other sads-derived entries.

## Model

$$
p(x)=\frac{x^{-s}e^{-\omega x}}{\operatorname{Li}_s(e^{-\omega})},
\qquad x=1,2,\dots
$$

Pueyo's power-bend in ecology (`sads::dpowbend`), the discrete power law with
exponential cutoff of Clauset, Shalizi and Newman (2009) elsewhere. It is a
two-parameter exponential family with natural parameters $(-s,-\omega)$ and
sufficient statistics $(\sum\log x_i,\sum x_i)$, so the log-likelihood is
concave in $(s,\omega)$. The continuous analogue is the gamma.

Parameter set: $\omega>0$ with any real $s$, plus the face $\omega=0$, $s>1$
(the zeta law). sads restricts to $s\ge0$; that is not an existence
restriction and should not be copied (rule R5 of the
[parameter rules](2026-10-29-parameter-rules.md)).

| slice or face | law | family here |
|---|---|---|
| $\omega=0$, $s>1$ | zeta | `zeta` (escape, "use `mlzeta`") |
| $s=0$ | geometric on $\{1,2,\dots\}$, prob $1-e^{-\omega}$ | `ztgeom` |
| two adjacent support points | two-point law on $\{k,k+1\}$ | none: no MLE |

## ML behaviour (derivation; numerical check below)

- **Nonexistence.** The points $(\log x,x)$ lie on a convex curve, so the
  boundary of the convex support is the polyline of chords between consecutive
  points, plus the vertical ray above $(0,1)$. Samples taking only the values
  $k$ and $k+1$ (and single-valued samples) put the statistic on that
  boundary; the supremum is the empirical two-point law and is not attained.
- **Escape to zeta.** The family is not steep at $\omega=0$ for $s>2$, where
  $E X=\zeta(s-1)/\zeta(s)$ is finite. By concavity, KKT at the face is
  sufficient: the MLE is the zeta fit iff the zeta MLE $\hat s_\zeta>2$ and
  $\bar x\ge\zeta(\hat s_\zeta-1)/\zeta(\hat s_\zeta)$. For
  $\hat s_\zeta\le2$ the $\omega$-score at the face is $+\infty$.
- **Interior.** Otherwise the statistic is interior and the MLE should exist
  with $\omega>0$; to be proved for this non-steep family (Barndorff-Nielsen
  1978, ch. 9, is the starting point).

Checked 2026-11-12 at 40 digits (high-precision comparison, not proof) in
[`powbend_check.py`](../research/evaluations/2026-11-12-sads-survey/powbend_check.py):
an escape sample ($\hat s_\zeta=2.761$, $\bar x=2.408\ge1.546$) whose profile
decreases in $\omega$ from $0$; an interior sample with MLE
$s=-0.2503$, $\omega=0.7542$ (outside sads's box); and a $\{3,4\}$ sample whose
log-likelihood rises to $10\log\tfrac12$ along a ray.

## Work

- **Polylogarithm.** Boost.Math has none. $\operatorname{Li}_s(e^{-\omega})$
  for real $s$ and $\omega>0$: the series $\sum k^{-s}e^{-\omega k}$ is fine
  for moderate $\omega$; small $\omega$ needs the expansion
  $\Gamma(1-s)\,\omega^{s-1}+\sum_k\zeta(s-k)(-\omega)^k/k!$ with integer-$s$
  limits. The fitter needs $\partial_s$ and $\partial_\omega$ (the latter is
  $-\operatorname{Li}_{s-1}$). This is the main numerical cost, and a
  `special/` seam other families could use.
- Tail-accurate d/p/q/r against mpmath; fitter by Newton on the concave
  problem with the face test above; dossier with the escape and
  nonexistence classification.

## Why

Small, structurally clean, and it exercises escape, nonexistence and a nested
limit family in one model, all with proofs within reach: material for the
[ML-difficulties paper](2026-11-02-ml-difficulties-paper.md). `sads::fitpowbend`
runs L-BFGS-B in the hard-coded box $s\in[0.1,2.999]$,
$-\log\omega\in[1,16]$ and reports a box edge as the fit, which is the failure
mode the library exists to rule out.
