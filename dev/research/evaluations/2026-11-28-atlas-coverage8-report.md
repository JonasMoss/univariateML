## Grading decisions

- **Generalized and nonstandard beta.** Held endpoint and first shape give the exact nonconstant attainment criterion. The compact superlevel and constant rate statements cover the exclusion and likelihood-divergence parts of failure. The rate statement does not show distributional concentration for arbitrary divergent paths. The fixed-two-shape failure join gives the extended-real contact, strict-support spike, compact nonconstant branch and constant collapse under a fixed beta coupling. The held-power uniqueness theorem covers every attained fit. Its regular proof uses finite digamma differences and score comparison, so it supplies the atlas cardinality conclusion without formalizing the profile-concavity attribution in the atlas prose. In nonstandard beta, the joined uniqueness theorem includes the finite-contact condition. The existing attainment criterion supplies positive, nonconstant inward distances and a free-end shape at least one. Reflection transfers the same width problem to either held endpoint. The unit-width result covers only the unit free-end shape. Conditional concavity in the other shape at a fixed width supplies a helper. [Generalized-beta declarations](2026-11-28-lean-genbeta-report.md)
- **Generalized-beta endpoint results.** The raw trace comparison assumes the slack tends to zero. The later divergence iff obtains that necessity in the subunit branch. A uniform upper bound away from contact there does not control the branch with upper shape at least one when other parameters vary. The $q=1$ ridge classifies only that slice, and the held-endpoint power-face improvement assumes $q>1$ and fixes a positive comparison rate. It rules out that face at that rate without providing the fitted gamma comparison or excluding the moving-endpoint power face. [Endpoint trace](../../lean/UnivariateML/Family/GBEndpointTrace.lean) · [Held-endpoint comparison](../../lean/UnivariateML/Family/GBHeldScaleShape1.lean#L661)
- **GB2 tree.** The transformed-gamma and inverse results compare a feasible witness with the best two boundary heights, preserving equality. They do not confine the differentiated profile score or supply a finite stationary search. Uniform exterior envelopes control the optimized likelihood at the two power ends but do not identify every nuisance-parameter escape. The GB2 peak, mode and tail-rate bounds are inputs to the boundary proof. The moving gamma and cutoff paths give one direction of the failure claim. The cutoff construction recovers full likelihood at tied contacts along its specified slack, with no bound on arbitrary drifting contacts. Feller–Pareto transfer requires positive excesses, exactly the scope of these atlas rows. The transfer identity alone supplies no stationary inventory or new escape classification. [GB2-tree declarations](2026-11-28-lean-gb2tree-report.md)
- **Tukey lambda.** The six joined criterion and failure claims for shape plus location, shape plus scale, and scale alone have full grades. The chart includes both fitted shape endpoints at held scale and treats a zero-likelihood sample as incompatible when support cannot yield positive likelihood. The fixed-location collapse theorem retains every tied observation in the critical coefficient and proves a strict finite-scale inequality. It does not give a moving-location envelope. The majority theorem uses the fitted shape $-1$, the unrestricted theorem is a separate clause, and the held-location corner theorems construct divergent paths. In particular the finite-critical joint path selects sequences and does not classify every sequence in that corner. The nonpositive inverse and continuity results supply profile construction, not uniqueness of its maximum. [Tukey lambda declarations](2026-11-28-lean-tlambda-report.md)
- **PGW and fatigue.** The PGW stationary-list theorems prove section attainment and nuisance elimination before applying analytic continuation and the root count. The all-free bijection identifies all finite winners even at face equality. The shape-held result supplies the retained list and Gompertz comparison, but has no a.e. conclusion and states no analogous winner bijection. The all-free failure join identifies optimized winning subsequences on nonattaining data and proves unboundedness on constants along a concentrating ray. The weaker witness, bounded-shape, large-shape and competitive-subsequence statements leave the stationary test, face optimization or selection of a winning subsequence to later results. The Gompertz tail and threshold contact lemmas cover the particular limits in their hypotheses. The fatigue join exhausts competitive subsequences and distinguishes the profiled threshold envelope from the likelihood along arbitrary shape drift. Its two constant mechanisms and the exponent $n-3j$, counting every tied minimum, match the atlas. The standalone envelope attains only at each nonsingular threshold separately. [PGW and fatigue declarations](2026-11-28-lean-pgw-report.md)

The abstract PGW lemmas assume the local analytic score and invertible nuisance derivative, or the analytic profile, nonzero analytic score and compact retained window, as appropriate. The monotone collar lemma assumes the derivative is already nonzero there. The chart extension assumes local analytic representatives at every nonnegative inverse exponent. These are helpers. `faces_le_of_upper_bound` assumes an upper bound over the full physical domain. `maximizer_bijection` starts from maximizing exponents and proves their stationarity, without establishing attainment, a finite root window or the face comparison. In the joined proofs, those obligations are handled by the section bounds, Hessian or scalar derivative, and strict far-tail comparison. No full grade here rests on a hypothesis of global attainment or an unproved competing-pair chart.
