# Numerical routine inventory

Updated 2026-10-31 for TASK-101.7 phase A at source base
`91d538253b6c448be78eedadde8abd9c44fb99c6`, plus the beta-header relocation.
The original 2026-10-30 source index was at
`5f11fb9e878219ac47a9a372e985eb69da6198e3`; its unresolved numerical questions
remain relevant, but its user counts and GB2 placement are superseded here.

The reproducible [98-family call-path table](2026-10-31-numerical-call-paths.md)
and [machine-readable source index](2026-10-31-numerical-call-paths.json) are
generated by `python3 dev/validation/numerical_inventory.py`. They record
family-level direct standard and seam calls, Boost targets expanded through named shared-function bodies
and explicit family delegates, own-helper symbol nominations, local headers, shared
include closure and source locations of scalar-solver/tail-helper nominations.
This is a lexical inventory, not a compiler-verified dynamic call graph:
inline/template dispatch, unqualified calls and delegation must be followed
in the listed source files. A header's availability does not prove execution.

The [phase A report](2026-10-31-step7-report.md) contains measured decisions
and a per-family solver proposal table. The
[shared-routine contract note](2026-10-31-step7-routines.md), linked from the
headers, records problem, range, initialization, fallback, stopping behavior,
accuracy evidence and concrete validation gaps. These notes distinguish
proved real-arithmetic facts from sampled floating-point checks.

Keep Boost provisionally. The seam includes both Boost wrappers and custom
log-tail continuations; it must not be described as entirely Boost-backed.
Lambert W replacement is proposed, not applied: speed alone does not justify
branch-point behavior changes. Scalar family changes are phase B only.
`fs_log1mexp` already shares the tail primitive; no second identical body
within special requires consolidation. Family duplicates are not authorized
for replacement during the concurrent diagnostics job. The beta bridge moved
without numerical changes, verified against baseline truth evaluations.

Earlier evidence remains linked above and in the shared-routine note; neither
historical passes nor current goldens constitute an all-input error bound.

Sampler paths are callback-dependent: generic `r*` templates ask the supplied
RNG for primitive draws. The standalone
[`StdRng`](../../../univariateML-cpp/include/cppunivariate/distributions/rng_std.hpp)
uses `std::uniform_real_distribution`, `normal_distribution`,
`gamma_distribution`, `student_t_distribution` and integer-width-dependent
`poisson_distribution`; transformations and rejection schemes remain in family
headers. R RNG state is owned by bindings, not this C++ inventory. The generated
JSON also records standard/Boost call tokens per reachable header so that
indirect implementation paths can be inspected separately from direct calls.

The step-7 source review distinguishes 46 local scalar fitting-root owners and
13 local continuous distribution-inversion owners (nine overlap; 50 distinct
families). Shared solvers, multidimensional Newton restricted to one free
coordinate, objective-only minimization and integer lattice searches are
separate categories. The per-owner proposal tables and counting definitions
are in the phase A report; source-marker counts alone do not establish them.
