`E(c,u)` means estimate (unique defaults to true), `L(c)` limit, `N(reason)` nonexistence, `B` budget, and `F(reason)` failure. The certification column defines `c`: **flag** means `r.certified`; **dossier yes** means true from `fit.certified: "yes"`. `p` is GED’s `proved_tie`. For Feller–Pareto, `g` is `r.certified` and at least one fitted coefficient not NaN: the generic margin-based face comparison returns all four coefficients NaN, whereas delegate faces retain coefficients. Half-t normal escapes use `L(false)` because their selection uses a likelihood margin. These are existing certification claims, not new mathematical certificates.
