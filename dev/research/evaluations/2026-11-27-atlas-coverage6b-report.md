## Incidence and envelope hypotheses

`UniqueAE.ae_winners_derivative` gets equal sample derivatives at attained winners using compact pieces and a countable cover of the feasible graph. The family proofs supply the open graph or work with a locally closed parameter subtype on a product chart, and prove likelihood continuity, continuity of the sample derivative and differentiation in the physical levels. Positive counts cancel coordinatewise. This result leaves score separation to its caller. It compares finite winners and does not supply attainment or compare arbitrary boundary traces. [Envelope and cover](../../lean/UnivariateML/Generic/UniqueAE.lean#L261)

For analytic incidence the parameter coordinates describe both competing candidates. The wrappers partition by the finite vector of multiplicity indices in `Fin n → ℕ`. On each stratum the equation at level i takes the derivative just before the first nonzero one. Joint analyticity supplies smoothness of those derivatives. Each equation depends only on its own sample coordinate and the pair parameters, so the sample block is diagonal with nonzero entries. AE1Incidence constructs local implicit graphs, takes a countable subcover on each relative stratum and proves each image null using Hausdorff dimension. AE3 obtains the finite orders from nonidentity on a connected common interval. AE4 takes nonzero germs directly and obtains them in its family wrapper from analytic score identification. A pointwise finite set of roots for each fixed pair, without this projected-null step, is insufficient. [AE1Incidence](../../lean/UnivariateML/Generic/AE1Incidence.lean#L69), [AE3AnalyticIncidence](../../lean/UnivariateML/Generic/AE3AnalyticIncidence.lean#L43), [AE3WeightedChart](../../lean/UnivariateML/Generic/AE3WeightedChart.lean#L132), [AE4Analytic](../../lean/UnivariateML/Generic/AE4Analytic.lean#L52)
