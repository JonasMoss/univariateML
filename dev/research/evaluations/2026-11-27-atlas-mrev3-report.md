## Proof and scope errors

- **Tukey lambda all-free uniqueness.** The existing all-free atlas row cites the uniform plateau and isolated reflected tie from the held-scale regime. With all parameters free, the exact pair \(0,10^{20}\) in that cited example has the unique range uniform by the pair inequality. The replacement records the proved equally replicated two-level singleton. [Held-scale claim](2026-11-25-tl-math-report.md#exact-held-scale-uniqueness-and-nonuniqueness-with-free-shape) · [All-free pair inequality](2026-11-21-tlambda-ties-report.md#distinct-pairs)

- **Beta-binomial all-free uniqueness, a separate existing key.** The current row says the size profile has tied global modes on specified examples. Its cited sample \(7,8,13\) has a strict local peak at size 38, which the finite size-13 witness beats. The cited report explicitly leaves ties between attained global sizes open. No proposal replaces that key. [Size comparison](2026-11-25-bbinomfree-math-report.md#nonunimodality-and-exact-ties)

- **Integer maximizer procedures.** The four proposed exact uniqueness grades for hypergeometric and negative hypergeometric rely on procedures for computing the set. These rows carry the required one-sided grade, retain the procedures and record exact saturated samples with multiple maximizers. The attainment grades remain iff because the tail tests terminate and the comparisons are rational or separate provably unequal values.

- **Skew-t normalization error in another mask.** The prior held-slant and held-location crossing multiplied its objective by an extra power of scale. Its checker can verify that altered crossing without establishing a likelihood tie. The all-free reflected witness uses the different normalization \(R^4\pi^{20}L\) and its own direct density comparison. [Earlier review](2026-11-27-atlas-mrev1-report.md#proof-errors) · [All-free reflected witness](2026-11-27-st-atlas-math-report.md#reflected-finite-maxima-with-unrestricted-slant)

- **GPD domain.** The held-shape result uses the fitted free-scale domain \(\xi\ge-1\). The old \(\xi<-1\) endpoint claim refers to a different domain. Under the reference convention R7a its infinite density is attained at the included contact, while the fitted route rejects that hold. [Held-scale branch](2026-11-27-crit-open-math-report.md#gpd-held-muxi) · [Parameter rules](../../roadmap/2026-10-29-parameter-rules.md)

- **GB2 and skew-t exact-real comparisons.** The stationary conditions select a set of possible witnesses. Their iff grades use proved joint closure and the existence of an actual finite point meeting the face height. Neither proof needs a finite enumeration or a general exact numerical equality test. The high-precision GB2 replay only spot-checks the formulas.
