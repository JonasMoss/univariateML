## Mathematics: proved

- Fixed finite alpha: strict concavity in inverse scale and scaled location;
  fixing xi and/or omega imposes affine constraints. Newton solves each
  constrained score; there is no generic 3D optimizer in the fitter.
  With xi fixed and alpha free, beta=alpha/omega separates the objective:
  scale is exact RMS about xi and shape solves a monotone scalar score.
- Normal profile stationarity for every unrestricted nonconstant sample;
  proportional direct location/shape scores and singular Fisher information.
- Both half-normal face suprema, including fixed scales and locations.
  Fixed-xi observations exactly at xi retain a log(2) penalty each.
  With xi inside the sample range neither infinite-shape face is feasible;
  xi on/outside the range leaves monotone free-shape escape.
- Centred mean/SD/skewness has nonsingular first-order Fisher information at
  symmetry via a transformation singular in direct shape; this does not
  remove infinite ML estimates or guarantee all higher derivative regularity.

## Mathematics: assumed / limits of numerical evidence

- A finite central delta grid and logarithmic wings through |alpha|=2^32,
  refining every sampled local maximum, locate the relevant interior modes.
  This is not certified root isolation of every possible profile stationary
  point. Exact faces win ties within 1e-8*n.
- Scaled 8-point Gauss–Legendre quadrature (80 panels, transformed distance
  0 to 40) in CDF cancellation regions has no uniform certified error bound.
- No penalised estimator is implemented. The note distinguishes papers read
  fully from bibliographic/abstract checks and flags the later arXiv revision.
