
## Implementation audit

None of the target family-and-hold combinations has an implemented truncation route in the modifier atlas, for known or estimated cuts. The design lists Poisson, geometric, normal, binomial, held-size negative binomial and finite-window zeta, and none is in the target set. The R dispatch refuses unsupported truncation before resolving NA cuts. The shared binding dispatch likewise rejects these families. For example `mldgamma(c(2,3), truncate=c(NA_real_,NA_real_))` encounters the documented `truncate` refusal, although on the mathematical design both cells attain their empirical Bernoulli with a continuum of parent fits. This is an availability gap documented by the interface. There is no implemented target status to contradict and hence no status-disagreement sample. ## What this changes for the fitter

A positive-mass tight-window solve can reuse the compatible fixed-window criterion, conditional on the realized cuts. A moving support must be clipped at the trial coefficients. At an occupied closed endpoint the inside density supplies the sample factor. The plateau condition then recovers the complete estimated-cut fibers from the winning parent vectors. In particular a fixed all-held model can have more than one maximizing nominal cut. Dgamma can report the sample extremes as canonical representatives and record that real-valued cuts remain nonunique.

On constant continuous data, test for expansion at a null tight slice before declaring an empty event. In particular a fixed opposite cut beyond an occupied parent endpoint can leave a positive nominal width but zero mass at the tight cut. When a permitted expansion gives a positive finite density at the observation, report the divergent point-mass limit rather than returning the zero-width fit. Distinguish singular endpoint densities, support incompatibility and certified finite nonattainment. For a positive-width search with an unresolved certificate, the wrapper's successful-search fallback retains the finite candidate with the uncertified flag. Proven divergence or a refuted candidate requires the corresponding stop. [Wrapper failure rule](../../validation/diagnostics.md#wrapper-failure-handling-rule-r6)
