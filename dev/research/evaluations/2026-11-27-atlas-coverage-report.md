## Where the statements stop

- **EMG.** `critical_value_criterion` assumes a function continuous on the closed interval, differentiable in its interior, and equal there to the actual profiled likelihood. It compares the resulting abstract endpoint values. Applying it to the all-free claim requires constructing that extension, identifying its endpoint values with the optimized normal and translated exponential faces, and proving the regularity hypotheses. `fixed_shape_fit` closes the inner optimization. The five held-mask `*_criterion` results are genuine comparisons for their own masks, with a nonzero residual needed for `held_location_criterion`. Their top-level atlas cells are open. The single-point supremum results for held rate and/or scale require a separate product argument to carry over to arbitrary samples. [EMG declarations](../../lean/UnivariateML/Family/SkewLSEMG.lean)
- **Half-t.** `joint_attainment_criterion` proves the face comparison under the at-most-half minimum multiplicity hypothesis. Its witness ranges over eligible finite shapes without requiring a stationary shape or the feasible floor. `joint_majority_unbounded` supplies the complementary divergence branch. A Lean statement joining them and relating the witness to the stationary/floor candidate set would close the exact all-free criterion. `scale_root_normal_limit` assumes an eventually positive score-root path. `profiled_half_normal_limit` implements the extra root step in the profiled result. `held_endpoint` and `held_shape` state an equivalence for existence of a *unique* maximum. The failure results and the existence statements for the fixed-shape masks still need to be assembled at the level of the exact claims. The `nu+sigma` endpoint theorem establishes a unique maximum on a closed support interval, while the corresponding atlas claim remains open. [Half-t declarations](../../lean/UnivariateML/Family/SkewLSHalfT.lean)
- **Feller–Pareto.** `narrow_span_moment_bound` assumes four quantitative inequalities, including the lower bound on concentration and the two comparisons involving the sample and shape. The downstream `held_power_narrow_span_no_mle` discharges the ingredients for the narrow-span, large-third-moment exclusion. Its free scale and held power scope has no exact `min+shape2` mask in the atlas. The existing `min` mask permits the power to vary, so the theorem has no coverage entry there. A scoped claim would first need that mask and its precise sample predicate. [Feller–Pareto declarations](../../lean/UnivariateML/Family/ShapeFacesFellerPareto.lean)
- **Skellam and Conway–Maxwell–binomial.** `attained_of_uniform_tail` assumes continuity, a positive witness and a uniform tail bound for an abstract likelihood. `joint_attained` proves these for Skellam and gets full attainment coverage. The equal-rate comparison is within a fixed-product slice, and `joint_unique_one_magnitude` only covers balanced samples supported on a single opposite pair. For CMB, `exposed_mass` and `exposed_supremum` assume the exposing slope and its support geometry. The specialized finite-size results address particular faces. They leave the moment-hull interior and closed seams of the joint fit, and the complete held-probability and held-dispersion classifications. [Skellam declarations](../../lean/UnivariateML/Family/Discrete3Skellam.lean) · [CMB declarations](../../lean/UnivariateML/Family/Discrete3CMB.lean)
- **Poisson–inverse Gaussian.** The positive joint theorem works in the open Bessel chart. The chart bijection is an auxiliary coordinate result. The positive-count held-mean theorem includes the Poisson equality branch. Its inward score is `n/μ` times variance plus squared displacement minus sample mean. Both criteria and cardinality claims also involve the all-zero seam. The binary theorem establishes a strict face inequality on binary counts. The limit detector also mentions a floating-point fallback, outside those real-arithmetic statements. [PIG declarations](../../lean/UnivariateML/Family/Discrete3PIG.lean)
- **Beta-negative binomial.** `tilted_score_neg` and `score_root_unique` assume a one-crossing sign pattern and integrability for an abstract integrand. The specialized one-dimensional fit theorems furnish the positive-count branches, and `all_zero_held_faces` treats their complement. `logLikelihood_swap` proves the stated symmetry implication for an off-diagonal maximizer. Proving a particular sample has an off-diagonal global maximizer would require a separate global comparison. [Beta-negative-binomial declarations](../../lean/UnivariateML/Family/Discrete3BNBinom.lean)
- **Shape and face theorems.** Inverse Burr's `joint_maximizers` is a bijection of maximizing sets. It gives a transfer principle and leaves exhibition of an exact tied Burr sample to a source theorem. For held power the Lomax criterion is a genuine equivalence, and `held_power_face` proves dominance under its negation. The optimized exponentiated Weibull face theorems construct paths to separately optimized boundary values. A global criterion still needs bounds on all escaping sequences, plus finite/face comparisons. `held_shape_attains` establishes existence for nonconstant data and leaves uniqueness open in Lean. The fixed exponent-one theorems are specializations, and the Gompertz exponential seam lemmas only give local comparisons or a held-shape optimum. [Inverse Burr declarations](../../lean/UnivariateML/Family/ShapeFacesInverseBurr.lean) · [Exponentiated Weibull declarations](../../lean/UnivariateML/Family/ShapeFacesExpWeibull.lean)
- **Skew normal, Bhattacharjee and slash.** The moving half-normal paths establish compatible products, while `half_normal_envelope_upper` controls paths with a finite location–scale limit. Joint attainment needs an exhaustive escape bound and the comparison with the optimized faces. The positive-width Bhattacharjee theorem omits the zero-width normal member, and held-scale attainment leaves the variance cut and exact tied widths to separate proofs. The slash paired collapse criterion needs a sample built from blocks with two centre observations and a symmetric opposite pair. The general half-multiplicity criterion still needs the other sample configurations and comparison of all finite and collapse candidates. [Skew-normal declarations](../../lean/UnivariateML/Family/SkewLSSN.lean) · [Bhattacharjee declarations](../../lean/UnivariateML/Family/SkewLSBhatt.lean) · [Slash declarations](../../lean/UnivariateML/Family/SkewLSSlash.lean)
