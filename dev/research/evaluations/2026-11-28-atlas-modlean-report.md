## Scope of the generic results

- The mapped `*_bounded_attainment` and `*_bounded_fit` results omit singleton windows. An iff for a **unique** maximizer does not by itself rule out every maximizer when its right-hand side fails. In the unrestricted finite chart, the needed strictness or endpoint exclusion can be imported from the generic proof. The `*_lower_atom` statements give pointwise masses along one end. They contain neither a likelihood maximum nor a comparison with the other end. [Finite fits and limits](../../lean/UnivariateML/Family/TruncCount.lean#L576)
- The `*_zt_fit` and `*_zm_fit` names can suggest a formula in public coefficients. Their existential conclusions generally assert cardinality in a chosen chart. Geometric reciprocal mean and NB mean matching belong in explicit identification theorems when the atlas asks for them. The `*_zm_fit` declarations also assume at least one positive observation. [Zero-modifier statements](../../lean/UnivariateML/Family/TruncZero.lean#L38) · [Tagged count statements](../../lean/UnivariateML/Family/TruncCount.lean#L766)
- `poisson_zi_boundary` is a log-MGF inequality on feasible increments, with a prescribed ordinary mean rate and a zero-fraction inequality. It contains no `IsMaxOn` statement on the public inflation chart. The bridge needs the change of variables and normalized likelihood identity. The Poisson and positive-size NB `*_zi_positive_attainment` declarations provide existence on a compact weight set with a witness below one. They state neither the maximizing weight or rate nor the all-zero case. `cmb_inflated_unique` solves one real-valued objective on an explicitly constrained chart, with no parametric conclusion for other dispersions, probabilities, histograms or held weights. [Inflation statements](../../lean/UnivariateML/Family/TruncZero.lean#L366)
- `lower_window_fit` assumes summability throughout the chart and a right-hand barrier above the value at each tilt. The family application must prove the barrier. `finite_exists_iff` assumes a finite set with positive carrier and the whole natural-parameter space. Conditional convex support still has to be computed, and a constrained chart needs its own boundary comparison. `factorized_max_iff` and `modified_max_iff` assume nonnegative factors and a positive witness. `modified_unique` additionally takes the conditional unique maximum as a premise. These are reduction theorems for a supplied likelihood and domain. [Lower-window theorem](../../lean/UnivariateML/Family/TruncCount.lean#L88) · [Finite existence and factorization](../../lean/UnivariateML/Generic/Truncation.lean)
- `fixed_carrier_inflation_boundary` takes normalization, first moment and exponential summability as hypotheses. `concave_boundary_max` takes concavity and a nonpositive derivative in every feasible inward direction. Both need a family-specific chart and regularity proof. An inequality on the ordinary boundary leaves the interior optimum and comparison across size, shape or other moving carriers open. [Boundary results](../../lean/UnivariateML/Generic/Truncation.lean#L523)
