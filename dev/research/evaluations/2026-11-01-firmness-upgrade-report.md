## Corrections and open questions

- The GEV dossier formerly asserted that the global supremum at half-minimum ties is approached only along collapse while also allowing an interior point to beat it. The path gives a finite limit; global comparison and exhaustion of joint paths remain open. The free Fréchet equality likewise needs a certificate of the best finite competitor and of relevant joint paths. The GEV held-location path is now calculated for each fixed positive shape; free shape and the global comparison remain open.
- SGED records 1–2 need an optimization of coupled uniform endpoint rates with the stated holds and endpoint observations; record 31 is a numerical search guard and needs a tail certificate for the search, not merely a limiting density. It remains numerical.
- SSTD records 8, 14, 19, 23, 27, 32, 36, 42, 45, 49 and 52 are equality diagnostics. Their fixed-shape collapse exponent establishes a finite path value at equality, but does not settle every joint rate, attainment, and global comparison under each held subset. They remain numerical. Density convergence of records 53–58 is proved away from endpoints; exact endpoint likelihood values and the numerical comparison are separate.
- The generalized-beta gamma face is on `scale / x` (or gamma on `-log(x/scale)`), not gamma on `x/scale`. Its path now fixes the second shape and specifies a positive product; the power face now specifies a positive limiting product and a lower bound on the second shape. The SSTD t2 record now states the finite ordinary-scale path; holding sd fixed cannot reach it.
- The two unchanged argued records, lst 3 and std 3, need an attainment and finite-competitor analysis at fixed-df tie equality. No numerical evidence has been promoted to proof.
