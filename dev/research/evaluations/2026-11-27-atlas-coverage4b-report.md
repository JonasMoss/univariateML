## SGED held-mean counterexample

Let \(p=\nu\), \(s=\mathrm{sd}\), \(a=\sqrt{\Gamma(1/p)/\Gamma(3/p)}\), \(k=\Gamma(2/p)/\Gamma(1/p)\), and \(b>0\) be the skewed unit-base standard deviation returned by sged_sigma. Write \(H=\xi+\xi^{-1}\). The dossier GED exponent is \((|v|/a)^p\): its scale written with \(2^{-1/p}\) cancels the factor \(1/2\) in the exponent. The standardized C++ coordinate is \(z=b(x-\mu)/s+ak(\xi-\xi^{-1})\). It uses \(v=z/\xi^{-1}\) on the left and \(v=z/\xi\) on the right. The applied Jacobian is \(b/s\) and the skew multiplier is \(2/H\). [SGED dossier](../../../univariateML-cpp/include/cppunivariate/distributions/sged/README.md) · [Density code](../../../univariateML-cpp/include/cppunivariate/distributions/sged/dpqr.hpp) · [GED scale and skew standardization](../../../univariateML-cpp/include/cppunivariate/special/skewed_densities.hpp)

Set \(w=1/(1+\xi^2)=\xi^{-1}/H\) and \(u=b/(aHs)\). The left and right kernel widths are \(w/u\) and \((1-w)/u\), and the mode is \(\mu-k(1-2w)/u\). Thus \(z/(aH)=(x-\mu)u+k(1-2w)\). The Lean residual divided by its side width fraction equals \(v/a\). The symmetric normalizer is \(1/(2a\Gamma(1+1/p))\), using \(\Gamma(1+1/p)=\Gamma(1/p)/p\). Including the skew multiplier and Jacobian gives \(u/\Gamma(1+1/p)\) per observation. The Lean expression equals the dossier log likelihood, including its normalizing term. The positive scale maps bijectively to positive inverse width, and positive finite skew maps bijectively to \(0<w<1\).

For \(p=1/2\), \(k=6\). Let \(A=\max_{t>0}\log(1+t^2)/t>0\), attained by ratio_attains. The theorem constructs \(w=6/(A^2+12)\), hence \(\xi=\sqrt{1+A^2/6}>1\). For \(x=(-1)\) and held mean zero it proves two distinct global maximizing inverse widths and hence two distinct positive sd values. The dossier explicitly permits a supplied subunit shape. At \(p\ge1\) the penalty is convex in the affine inverse-width residual and \(n\log u\) is strictly concave. The companion theorem proves exactly one maximum when some residual is nonzero. All-zero residuals give unbounded collapse. [Counterexample](2026-11-27-lean-twopiece-report.md#heldmeanskew_subunittie) · [Lean statements](../../lean/UnivariateML/Family/TwoPieceSGED.lean)

The original atlas claim inferred unconditional uniqueness from the power-loss profile. Its cited certificate map distinguishes convex inverse scale from the subunit compact cover. Below shape one the moving join and concave powers within sign cells invalidate the convexity argument. The dossier already allows separated ties. The [replacement row](2026-11-27-atlas-coverage4b-data/proofs.csv) states the shape-at-least-one result and exact subunit witness. The shared atlas row remains pending the coordinator's replacement and is included in the counts and inventory as written evidence with known false wording.
