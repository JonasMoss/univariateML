## Where the declarations stop

- **Burr, inverse Burr and Pareto IV.** `jointMLE_iff` takes the optimizing Weibull power and intercept as supplied data, with an assumption that they bound all Weibull competitors. `weibull_optimized` supplies their existence. The physical, reciprocal and translated `*_criterion_and_escape` versions discharge that assumption for nonconstant positive data or strictly positive excesses. Their compactness conclusion controls superlevels strictly above the larger face. Burr's `large_shape_le_weibull` assumes a Weibull bound and permits a positive error of order `1/k`. `small_shape_lt_pareto` controls only small first shape. The transported majority theorems give strict Pareto or power dominance and the exact supremum when at least half the sample is at the relevant extremum. They leave the rest of the sample space and the fitter's arithmetic-margin detectors open. The Weibull path retains full coverage. [BurrFree](../../lean/UnivariateML/Family/BurrFree.lean)
- **Paralogistic and inverse paralogistic.** The diagonal fit and physical fit are nonconstant-data results. `paralogistic_two_level_tie` gives two actual finite global maximizers separated by a strict shape split, which suffices for the existential claim. The inverse theorem transfers that construction for its explicit two-level sample. The written reciprocity claim concerns preservation of exact all-free ties in general. A theorem on the whole maximizing set under reciprocation would cover that claim. The public constant-divergence declarations establish an unbounded image. Explicit physical-coordinate limits with diverging shape would close the path cells. [BurrFree](../../lean/UnivariateML/Family/BurrFree.lean)
- **EMG.** The earlier `critical_value_criterion` takes continuity on a compact interval, interior differentiability and agreement with the actual interior profile as hypotheses. `FinishEMG.compactification` supplies all three for nonconstant data. `tendsto_optimizer` assumes joint convergence, concavity, a strict limiting optimizer and an eventually competitive feasible point. `hasDerivAt_profile` assumes convergence of the nuisance optimizer and the two-sided comparison that identifies the profile derivative. These are auxiliary results. `normal_endpoint` and `exponential_endpoint` give limits of the optimized dimensionless-shape profile. ## Written-only claims by family and proof requirement

The table enumerates each post-review cell with status `written`. A bare held mask in the first three question columns denotes that regime's main claim. In the other columns, the keys in parentheses identify the exact atlas claim under that mask. The atlas gives the statement, written source and any partial Lean evidence.

- **Attainment** needs a finite fit and the converse across all sample branches, usually by compact superlevels or an explicit maximizer. Several families already have branch theorems and need a statement joining them.
- **Failure** needs the specified divergence, strict unattained supremum or uniform no-escape bound, including proper closed members and equality cases.
- **Cardinality** needs a strict comparison, a complete description of the maximizing set, or an exact tie, according to the claim. A normalization or transfer theorem is needed where existing results concern a different objective or chart.
- **Paths** need a feasible parameter sequence preserving the required holds and its joint sample limit. **Limit winners** need an upper envelope over all competitors and the claim's detector implication. For a numerical detector this includes the stated arithmetic assumptions.
- **Scoped cases** need the exact sample predicate and conclusion attached to the case key, which may include attainment of a boundary equality.
