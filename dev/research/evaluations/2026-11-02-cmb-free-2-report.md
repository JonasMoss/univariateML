## Mathematics and numerical certificate

For fixed `N >= 2`, the natural statistic `(x, log choose(N,x))` is minimal: the strictly concave sequence of log binomial coefficients gives affinely independent support points. The full natural-parameter log likelihood is strictly concave and has a finite maximum exactly when its empirical statistic is in the polygon interior. Consecutive support points form the upper edges, the endpoint pair forms the lower edge, and vertices represent point masses. The upper and lower edges give adjacent and endpoint two-point limits, respectively. At fixed interior probability the dispersion profile is strictly concave and has a finite maximum exactly when the empirical mean of the log binomial coefficient is strictly between its extremes. At `N=1` dispersion is unidentified. Thus the proposed exponential-family argument is valid, but a small numerical score alone does not prove a useful likelihood error bound.

The implementation uses damped Newton steps with score checks, followed by a global entropy-dual upper bound. The computed probability vector is corrected to match the empirical sufficient-statistic moments (one moment with held probability), and negative weights or failed moment checks reject the certificate. The dual inequality follows from nonnegative relative entropy. If Newton fails, a budget-reserved safeguarded bisection rescue must pass the same entropy check. Comparison of that upper bound with the candidate bounds the remaining fixed-size likelihood error. The truncated CMP-like two-coordinate profile is bounded in the same manner.

For samples spanning at least three integers, binary coarsening of the empirical saturated likelihood bounds relevant endpoint and intermediate probabilities on a candidate superlevel set. These bounds control positive and negative dispersion, and the dossier's binomial-coefficient correction supplies a uniform decreasing `O(1/N)` tail bound at fixed truncation. This covers CMP, geometric and escaping-mass paths without needing to classify which path dominates. For two adjacent observations with probability held, the two-outcome likelihood bounds their log-probability ratio, which bounds dispersion by a term decreasing as `1/log(N)`; a truncated geometric profile and correction then bound the tail. The odd-size central face is compared separately. With free probability the adjacent positive and endpoint samples have exact saturated face solutions; `{0,1}` has an attained Bernoulli maximum tied with an adjacent CMP limit. Further exact symmetric held-probability cases and the two-size comparison for `{0,1}` at probability 1/2 are described in the dossier.

These inequalities and hull classifications are proved in exact arithmetic, conditional on the computed comparisons. They are not outward-rounded interval certificates. Practical safeguards include the entropy allowance `1e-9*(1+n*abs(entropy))`, profile/face comparison scale `1e-8*(1+abs(logLik))`, moment residual checks, and conservative margins on the probability and curvature bounds; the dossier states the precise checks. Searches allow ten million charged support terms; nontrivial searches stop when the observed maximum exceeds 10000 or necessary truncation exceeds 4096. These limits are failure conditions, never returned estimates.

## Open limitations

- The golden sample and other cases such as the held-probability 0.3 example above still need tighter or more efficient cutoff/certification to return a certified answer within the work budget. The current diagnostic establishes that candidate and truncated-profile construction succeed for the golden sample, but does not isolate the later stopping step. It needs instrumentation of cutoff/enumeration and a reproducible improvement; increasing a cap alone would not certify an answer.
- The settled R wrapper generator cannot honestly encode an unnamed numerical face or an attained finite face tie in its current `limits` entries: one form emits a broken `ml()` link and the other asserts nonexistence. The CMB dossier's existence prose and wrapper diagnostics describe the tie accurately. A future generator change needs explicit support for unnamed numerical faces and attained ties, with regression tests; code generation was outside this task's permitted edits.
- The pre-existing held-probability positive constant-sample guard above five million remains outside the nonconstant-sample scope. The general free-size existence and uniqueness classification and a complete classification of joint limiting paths remain open; the implemented tail bound avoids needing the latter for certified successful searches.
