## 4. Closure lemma hypotheses by family

The TeX overview declares compactness, envelope regularity and tightness assumed at family level. The sources below often supply enough for the *local version* of the lemma, or an exact reduction that replaces it. The labels have the following meanings. `Profile` means the proof treats the boundary after an exact nuisance reduction or exhausts competitive sequences. `Local` means a compact competitive set with continuity or upper semicontinuity, in the stated scope. `Direct` means a global inequality or explicit optimizer proves the result without invoking compactification. `Partial` or `assumed` means the unrestricted claim needs additional closure analysis. Upper semicontinuity of the limsup envelope is automatic in the compact metrizable construction. What needs checking is upper semicontinuity on the feasible set and agreement of the joint boundary value with any claimed face. The continuous real extensions in the Lean generic lemmas have stronger regularity hypotheses than the extended-real TeX lemma. The proof for a compact superlevel does not establish global tightness of the laws.
