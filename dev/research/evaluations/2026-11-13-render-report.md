## Rendering and host conversion

[`umvl_binding::render_fit`](../../../bindings/common/fit.hpp) is the sole adapter-result renderer. It derives the public stop kind from `FitOutcome`, removes obsolete certification extras, selects an existing candidate for an estimate previously rendered as a tie, clears its stop message, and appends the required sentence to uncertified limits. It copies candidate coefficients and the selected candidate's lower likelihood bound without numerical evaluation. Selection is stable on equal lower bounds.

It is called by:

- R [`common_result`](../../../bindings/r/scripts/common_fit.h);
- JS [`fit_output`](../../../bindings/js/bindings.cpp);
- the C++ probe generated by [`fit-snapshot.py`](../../validation/fit-snapshot.py).

Adapters retain their diagnostic text and numerical payload construction. Their former kind literals are internal `PayloadKind` tags used to preserve validation and override decisions; these tags do not decide the rendered stop kind. `with_outcome` also records requested coefficient holds for comparison estimates, which formerly stopped before exposing that metadata. It does not manufacture a missing held coefficient. Both converters test the rendered kind, including when a diagnostic is empty.

R fully held, data-present fits previously evaluated their likelihood outside the adapters. The new internal [`ml_evaluated_result_`](../../../bindings/r/src/outcomes.cpp) passes those already computed coefficients and likelihood through the same renderer to obtain flags. Data-free fixed distributions carry the trivial fixed-coefficient flags. Censor conversion previously lacked an attached outcome despite the job's premise; [`censored_result`](../../../bindings/common/modifiers.hpp) now attaches one explicitly, preserving coefficients, likelihoods and diagnostics. Its remaining semantic gaps are listed below.

Host validation that fails before producing an adapter result retains its message and receives a classed invalid or unsupported condition/error. Existing R evaluation errors retain their evaluation subclass and gain numerical stop fields.

| Failure / conditioning | `conditioning` | `univariateML_conditioning`, `univariateML_numerical` |
| Other failure, including no-convergence and evaluation | `numerical` | `univariateML_numerical` |

Every condition ends with `univariateML_stop`, `error`, `condition`. Stops carry `kind` and the outcome's `reason`; limits also carry logical `certified`. JS errors carry the same fields. Existing candidates, limit coefficients, model names, accuracy details and likelihood bounds remain available. `common_fit.h` has no bare `Rcpp::stop` path.

Every estimate has logical `certified` and `unique` from its outcome: attributes on the R estimate vector and constructed object, and JS fields. `extra["certified"]` is no longer their source. R also preserves existing vector-valued descriptions as attributes. Comparison estimates retain `outcome_message`, their original comparison model and bounds for diagnostic inspection; the public estimate no longer raises that message as an error. Candidates are attached as an R data frame named `maximizers` when the outcome says non-unique, or `candidates` otherwise; JS uses corresponding arrays. Rows retain model names, all coefficient columns and lower/upper bounds.

An uncertified limit gains exactly this sentence, once:

> No improvement above the likelihood margin was found.

`print` and `summary` each add `Global maximum is not certified.` and/or `Maximum is not unique.` when the respective flag is false. They add neither line for a certified unique estimate. `model_select` accepts non-unique estimates and returns logical `certified` and `unique` columns. R `vcov`, `confint` and `bootstrapml`, and JS `vcov`, refuse `unique = FALSE` with an unsupported condition/error, reason `non-unique`, and the message `Inference requires a unique maximizer; this fit has multiple maximizers.`

The short [`univariateML_outcomes` help source](../../../bindings/r/R/outcomes.R) documents outcomes, reasons, flags and classes. The [2.0 NEWS entry](../../../bindings/r/NEWS.md) describes the change.
