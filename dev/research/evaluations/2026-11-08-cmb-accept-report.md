The new positive-held-dispersion block bound removes the census bottleneck. Write

```text
c_N(k) = sum_{j=0}^{k-1} log(1-j/N)
h_L(k) = -log(k!) + c_L(k)
a       = eta + nu*log(N)
```

For `L <= N <= R` and `K <= L`, the retained normalizer terms use `0,...,K`. Since `c_N(k)-c_L(k) >= 0` and `nu > 0`, their normalizer is at least the corresponding retained normalizer at `L`, at the same relaxed coordinate `a`. Their observed numerator correction is at most its value at `R`. Therefore every full profile in the block is bounded by

```text
truncated_profile_upper(L,K,nu)
    + nu * sum_i [c_R(x_i)-c_L(x_i)].
```

The scalar truncated likelihood is concave in `a`. Its derivative is `n*(empirical_mean-model_mean)` and has absolute value at most `n*K`. Bracketing the mean-matching root therefore supplies the upper allowance `n*K*bracket_width`. The implementation precomputes `h_L`, charges its cells and each normalizer actually evaluated, and stops when that bracket allowance is sufficiently small. An unexcluded integer block is bisected to checked singletons. This proves coverage without size unimodality and replaces the former ascending scan below the tail cutoff. It also removes the former worst-case reservation and repeated log-factorial calculation in the scalar truncation solve.

The scalar upper bound adds `1e-9*(1+abs(value))`; the block comparison adds `1e-9*(1+abs(anchor)+abs(correction))`. Solver passes use `min(iterlim,100000)`; all table and block work shares the unchanged `100000*min(iterlim,100000)` enumeration-cell allowance. Unsupported certificate truncations stop, and exploratory size proposals above the declared allocation domain stop before constructing a table. No allocation or work threshold becomes an estimate. These bounds are proved in exact arithmetic conditional on the computed comparisons. Normalizers, scores, moments, root brackets and comparisons are floating-point calculations with the stated practical margins. The implementation does not claim outward-rounded interval arithmetic or a complete classification of every joint limiting path.

There are explicit counterexamples to uniqueness:

- For observations `(1,1)` with `prob=1/2, nu=1`, sizes one and two both have likelihood `1/4`. In general `P_N(X=1)=N/2^N`, whose successive ratio is `(N+1)/(2*N)`. It equals one at `N=1` and is smaller thereafter, proving that these two finite sizes are global maximizers. Both witnesses are retained in a tie stop.
- With all-zero observations and free probability, or probability held at zero, every size admits the atom at zero. Likelihood one is globally maximal. Size-zero and size-one witnesses now produce a tie rather than an arbitrary size estimate.
- At held size one, dispersion is unidentified; at size zero both canonical coefficients are unidentified. Endpoint probabilities also make dispersion irrelevant. Their exact-law reductions select valid representatives of the maximizing set.
