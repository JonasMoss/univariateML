## Lomax

- **All free, nonconstant, exponential stop.** The analytic tails and interval cover use normalized likelihood. Cells are bounded below the target or ruled out as sites of a stationary maximum. If the queue closes without a winning root, the gap above the exponential is at most $10^{-8}$ per observation under the computed comparisons. The score and curvature gate uses $4\eta$, where (eta) is the magnitude- and sample-size-dependent arithmetic pad in the certificate. The exponential return stores the limiting coefficients and likelihood. The successful nonconstant margin stop does not export the global upper bound that the budget branch records separately. `firmness: proved` and `numerical: true` distinguish a proved limiting distribution from the numerical decision. `limit(false)` leaves strong nonexistence undecided. [Boundary metadata](../../../univariateML-cpp/include/cppunivariate/distributions/lomax/README.md#L31) · [Bound and decision](../../../univariateML-cpp/include/cppunivariate/distributions/lomax/certificate.hpp#L136) · [Outcome mapping](../../../univariateML-cpp/include/cppunivariate/distributions/lomax/mle.hpp#L320)
- **All free, constant.** The code detects equality of observations before running the cover and uses the same `limit(false)` status. For constant positive observations, $A(q)=\log(1+q)$. The normalized gain is strictly negative at every $q>0$ because $(1+q)\log(1+q)>q$, and tends to zero as $q\to0$. Here strong nonexistence is decided analytically. The shared limit flag loses that distinction. [Constant fast path](../../../univariateML-cpp/include/cppunivariate/distributions/lomax/certificate.hpp#L127)
- **All free, winning estimate.** Inside the winning box the signed endpoints and strict curvature establish one stationary maximum. Strict gain above the exponential, the analytic tail bounds and exclusion of competing cells establish its global uniqueness under the comparisons. The initial signed bracket is narrower than $7\times10^{-7}$ in log rate, and its saved limits are each widened by $10^{-7}$. Profiling gives a unique (kappa=1/A(q)) at that point. The public all-free overload invokes the Newton candidate again after the global certificate and propagates polish failure. The initial enclosure cannot on its own establish $10^{-10}$ coefficient accuracy. In the archived prototype replay, 22 all-free records contain a passing exact or interval gain, root, concavity and global quartet. The roots archive repeats those records. The quartet supports the global decision and isolated basin on those inputs. [Certificate](../../../univariateML-cpp/include/cppunivariate/distributions/lomax/certificate.hpp#L64) · [Public polish and mapping](../../../univariateML-cpp/include/cppunivariate/distributions/lomax/mle.hpp#L183) · [Prototype replay](2026-11-14-cert-replay-report.md)
- **Held subsets.** With (kappa) held, the log-rate score strictly decreases. The implementation requires computed signs outside its score allowance, a signed bracket of width at most `tol/2`, and represented log-rate restoration error at most `tol/4`. The dossier's reference to a $10^{-3}$ score threshold does not describe the current acceptance test. With (lambda) held, the shape is $n/\sum_i\log(1+\lambda x_i)$. With both held, the point is fixed. Inverse Pareto transfers those routes through reciprocals, and Pareto II through a threshold shift and reciprocal scale mapping. Pareto II has an additional threshold-observation path based on the sign of shape times the number of positive shifts minus the threshold multiplicity. ## Open

- **Route reachability and completeness.** The unit of static extraction is a return alternative. Some branches reuse a status across hold masks, and helpers can refine or replace a status. The missing evidence is a producer-to-return crosswalk showing the exact guard and mask for each unobserved alternative. In particular, the conservative expansion of shared comparison helpers can include alternatives unreachable for a particular family. The declared 86-family scope counts base fits. Modifier and censoring overloads are distinct routes and appear only where the replay index includes them.
- **Unclassified global and local evidence.** The ledger marks 377 global rows and 429 local rows unclassified. The missing items are identified by row: the exact status producer's collapse or face exclusion, the candidate-selection threshold, or a public-coordinate error bound following the final local acceptance. Dossier grades and replay comparison names alone cannot settle these. Dynamic certification members and typed-set construction also need separate proofs of their true and false cases where the row retains the member expression.
- **Replay promotion.** For claims beyond the verified Lomax example, an interval or exact archive entry labels a comparison. Promotion to an input-level conclusion requires grouping all prerequisites for the same source record, checking the audited revision retains the mathematics and classifying any holding/representation transformations. In particular, a replay of a rounded transformed sample needs a separate argument if the transformation changes the original input problem.
- **Numerical rounding versus approximation.** Several covers and final coefficient tests apply practical pads. The schema can distinguish a pad used to decide a sign from an allowable mathematical likelihood gap. For flagged cases in the family notes, the underlying data-specific uncertainty needs to be carried through the public restoration before interpreting the point as a verified maximizer.
