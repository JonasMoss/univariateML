## Earlier objections and remaining hypotheses

**Separation.** The two AE5 routes remove a supplied a.e. separation implication from the finite/finite comparison. `measure_ties_eq_zero` assumes pointwise identification from equal scores at any specified number of distinct common levels. Its proof provides the earlier weighted envelope's separation premise for every sample satisfying that count, then derives nullity for tied winners. Analyticity of the supplied score is used through continuity in this route. `ae_unique_of_identifiable_germs` instead assumes joint kernel analyticity, connected common support and a score difference somewhere in that support for each unequal pair. It also takes the inequality `m + m < n` as input. It supplies the incidence projection and yields at most one finite winner. Neither route makes score identification automatic. [AE5 generic statements](../../lean/UnivariateML/Generic/AE5Weighted.lean) · [Earlier objections, 6a](2026-11-27-atlas-coverage6a-report.md#almost-everywhere-uniqueness) · [Earlier objections, 6b](2026-11-27-atlas-coverage6b-report.md#what-the-statements-leave-open)

**Measure.** Both routes put Lebesgue measure on the free level coordinates, with multiplicities fixed first. Positive weights cancel in the winning-score equations. Restriction to ordered distinct-level chambers uses that same measure. The proof never transfers a null set from the ambient space of expanded observations. The continuous parameter term can absorb pinned observations, using a term divided by total free weight in the AE3 convention, which adds it to each weighted summand. Its continuity and an appropriate feasible chart still have to be established. [Weighted chart](../../lean/UnivariateML/Generic/AE3WeightedChart.lean)

**Attainment and competing heights.** The generic finite-winner results permit an empty maximizing set. The three pilot theorems discharge attainment on their a.e. scopes by identifying the weighted sum with the expanded log likelihood and applying the exact Lomax sign test, including equality with the excluded face. There is no assumed stationary list, attainment or competing-pair structure in the pilot declarations. The generic `ae_unique_with_competitors` assumes a finite index set and an a.e. height inequality in the form of non-equality to every attained finite winner. Its analytic-height hypothesis `_ha` is unused. The conclusion gives strictness only for competitors already at or below that winner. It proves neither attainment nor that all relevant faces are represented, below the candidate, or separated. Moving-contact comparisons still need composed heights, their derivatives and a nullity or exact-height argument. ## Incidence audit

`ae_no_implicit_incidence` assumes countably indexed strata, vanishing equations, local C¹ regularity and an invertible sample derivative. It constructs local implicit graphs, uses a countable relative subcover on each stratum and bounds the dimension of each differentiable graph image projected into sample space. AE3 and AE4 build the strata from vectors of zero orders. Each equation uses the derivative immediately before the first nonzero derivative. Joint analyticity gives smoothness, and each equation depends on only one sample coordinate at fixed pair. The nonzero next derivatives are the diagonal entries of the sample block. Connected common support and nonidentity give the finite orders in AE3. AE4 uses real analytic identity on the full line to turn global score identification into nonvanishing germs. `UniqueAE` constructs a separate countable cover by compact parameter pieces and sample balls for the winning-derivative comparison. Neither cover is a premise of the public family theorems below.
