## Inventory and interpretation

All existence reports present at the base were searched for `Disagreement`, `protected`, `fit.method`, `fit.note`, `numerical: true` and `Open`: October 31 batches 1–3, November 2 batches 4–7 and 9, the November 1 follow-up, and the October 31 open-existence and beta-binomial investigations. Batches 8, 10–12 and later batch reports were absent at this revision. Only 50 family dossiers had an `existence:` field; all edited dossiers belong to that set.

`numerical: true` records whether a detector uses a numerical search or likelihood comparison. It does not downgrade the independent `firmness` claim about the limiting path. Records that combine an analytic special case with a numerical general route are flagged because the record is not an exact-only detector. Direct constant-data, support and tie-count tests remain unflagged. In particular, the skew-t tie-equality guards remain exact data tests even where their mathematical interpretation is unresolved; their `firmness: numerical` alone does not require this flag.

Truncated-normal exponential and uniform records are flagged even though some branches use analytic moment criteria: the same statuses are also selected by the final `1e-8*n` likelihood preference, and the finite-interval tilt is solved numerically. GEV collapse records similarly include numerical half-tie comparisons alongside exact strict-collapse guards. Beta-prime held-shape records retain their analytic constant-sample exceptions while flagging the numerical general comparison.

Folded-normal certification now includes both the joint and held-positive-location convex-chord bounds, the monotone held-scale equation and the analytic zero-location scale. The bounds certify likelihood tolerance in exact arithmetic conditional on computed comparisons; this is not an outward-rounded error guarantee. Budget exhaustion stops. At the C++ default tol=1e-10, the chord-bound allowance is 1e-10*n. The final half-normal preference uses an additional `1e-11*n` likelihood margin. Beta-binomial remains partial: its note now records the actual tangent/chord bounds, supported integer tail bounds, numerical `1e-8` per-observation face preference and unsupported profiles. No existence theorem was inferred from a numerical test.

The patch changes 100 fields across 18 dossiers: 89 numerical flags and 11 fit fields. The GEV note/grade additionally identifies the exact xi=-1 reverse-exponential submodel and analytic location with xi=0 and scale held; the general profile remains uncertified. Logistic and truncated normal use the existing convex-solve category; shifted Gompertz, beta-binomial and GEV use profile-search.
