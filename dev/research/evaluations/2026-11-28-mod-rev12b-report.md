## Proof errors and scope corrections

### A null tight event can have positive nominal width

The shared profiling proof includes this branch, but the proposed collapse rows restrict it to nominal singletons. In Half-t hold `mu=0`, `nu=3`, `sigma=1`, observe `(0)`, and let the window be `[-1, NA]`. Tightening the upper cut to zero gives `[-1,0]` with effective intersection `{0}`. The tight conditional density is undefined. At upper cut `d>0` the effective interval is `[0,d]`. The kernel is decreasing there, so the conditional density at zero is at least `1/d`. The likelihood is unbounded and the conditional laws converge to the atom at zero. With lower estimation and fixed upper cut zero there is no rightward expansion at this held vector, so there is no positive-mass window. Any multiplicity of the observation raises the shrinking likelihood to the corresponding power. Opening the lower cut to `1-d` gives likelihood exactly `1/d` for `0<d<2`. Reflection gives the upper-estimated example with `(-1)` and design `[-2,NA]`. At shape `1/2` with the same location and scale, the upper support endpoint is 2 and has zero inward density. The sample `(2)` with design `[NA,3]` has zero likelihood on every compatible positive-width expansion at the held vector. Positive retained mass alone cannot justify a fit.
