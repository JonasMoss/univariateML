## Maximizer sets and implementation

The sets agree with the inventory and dossiers. Let $x$ denote the common observation and $B=2^{53}-1$ the largest supported urn count. Each set is intersected with the holds. Equality is decided from the mass function or its adjacent ratio. Reported log likelihoods use ordinary floating-point evaluation. [Inventory](2026-11-22-zero-unique-report.md)
