# Review of held zero modifiers in TASK-439

TASK-447 reviewed 42 proposals. The [join file](2026-11-27-mod-rev3-data/reviewed.csv) contains 41 rows: 35 accepted at the proposed grade and 6 regraded. It contains 24 held-`p0` rows and 17 held-`pi` rows, with 14 iff, 21 exact and 6 one-sided claims. One proposal was rejected. All entries carry origin `TASK-439`. Accepted claims have been edited where needed to spell out the hold, endpoints or comparison.

All 42 full keys are present in the modifier atlas. Removing the modifier coefficient gives an existing base regime in every case, using `all free` when the parent mask is empty. Binomial, geometric, negative binomial and Poisson are eligible by their integer support starting at zero and absence of a pre-existing zero construction. The former schema objection in the first review is resolved. ## Proof errors and grading corrections

- **The joint negative-binomial uniqueness claim is unsupported.** The held-inflation argument locates every positive-data finite maximizer among simultaneous score roots. Compactness above the Poisson face controls escaping sequences, but a compact set of common analytic zeros in two variables can contain a curve. The proof gives neither a count nor a component description of the winners on mixed samples. Attainment and the failure modes still follow from the boundary comparison. On all zeros the maximizing set is exactly the size-zero or probability-one union, with the entire admissible slice maximizing when `pi=1`. [Joint score proof](2026-11-27-trunc-expfam-math-report.md#nbinom-free-zeroinflated)
- **Finite stationary lists give one-sided cardinality coverage in these rows.** For free-size positive NB the nonzero Poisson factorial gap and the analytic extensions at both size ends ensure finiteness. With probability held in the interior the scalar size score is analytic at zero and eventually negative. For held-size inflated NB a positive-witness superlevel lies in a compact interior interval, where the nonconstant analytic objective gives finitely many stationary contenders. For held-size inflated binomial with positives and size at least two the likelihood polynomial vanishes at probability zero and is positive somewhere, so its derivative has finitely many roots. With size free, binomial has a finite candidate list per size and a finite winning prefix when a finite witness strictly exceeds the ZIP face. A general bound at finite-to-face equality is unproved. The modified-binomial rows use the earlier finite-prefix result for positive truncation, which supplies an exact maximizing set including finite size ties. [NB positive profile](2026-11-27-trunc-expfam-math-report.md#nbinom-free-zerotruncated) · [Held-size NB inflation](2026-11-27-trunc-expfam-math-report.md#nbinom-size-zeroinflated) · [Binomial inflation](2026-11-27-trunc-expfam-math-report.md#binom-free-zeroinflated) · [Positive-binomial finite decision](2026-11-25-tbinom-math-report.md#a-finite-decision-certificate)
- **The binomial free-inflation ridge does not establish a held-inflation tie.** For `(0,1)` the ridge at size one has `(1-pi)*prob=1/2`. Holding `pi=1/2` leaves just size one and probability one on the ridge. It is also the sole global winner under that hold, since larger sizes cannot assign exactly half to each of zero and one and no mass elsewhere. For ZIP the held-inflation tie is genuine. On 99 zeros and one ten the positive mean minus the overall mean is exactly `99/10>4`. The rate score has two peak branches between its turning levels. At the lower turning level the score is negative between the low-rate peak and the high-rate tangency, and at the upper level it is positive between the low-rate tangency and the high-rate peak. The peak-height difference increases strictly with inflation since the derivative of the zero term increases with rate and the positive-data derivative cancels. Its unique zero defines the exact hold and gives two global rates. The decimal locator is unnecessary for the proof. This refutation earns one-sided coverage. [Binomial ridge](2026-11-27-trunc-expfam-math-report.md#binom-free-zeroinflated) · [ZIP analytic crossing](2026-11-27-trunc-expfam-math-report.md#pois-free-zeroinflated)
- **The unrestricted shortcut from an infeasible hurdle winner to zero inflation is false.** In CMB, take `(0,2,2,2,2,2,2,2)`, hold `nu=1` and `prob=317/500`, and write `q=183/500`. At size two the conditional mass at two is highest but `q²>1/8`. At size three `q³<1/8`, so positive inflation fits the empirical zero share. Its exact rational likelihood exceeds the size-two boundary value. For each size at least three the next conditional mass at two is smaller by a ratio below `2q<1`, and the Bernoulli term cannot improve on size three. Thus size three with positive inflation is the unique global winner. The two math proposals use joint concavity only on fixed carriers where justified or retain cross-size or branch comparisons. The CMB rows in TASK-440 explicitly use both branches across designs and TASK-439 has no CMB row. [CMB proof](2026-11-27-zero-mod-math-report.md#cmb-all-free-inflated) · [TASK-440 proposals](2026-11-27-zero-mod-math-data/proofs.csv)

### Boundaries and equality

For modification the likelihood is the closed positive-window likelihood on `[1,Inf)` times `p0^n0 (1-p0)^n+`. The held Bernoulli factor must be positive. Any zero rules out `p0=0` and any positive rules out `p0=1`. With no positives the conditional factor drops out and the entire admissible parent slice maximizes, even when the held zero mass is strictly below one. With no zeros a held `p0<1` remains compatible and contributes the constant positive factor. Modified binomial uses positive sizes, including the flat conditional size-one law. The modified NB size-zero logarithmic law is admitted. Its ordinary inflation counterpart at size zero is a zero atom. [Factorization](2026-11-27-trunc-expfam-math-report.md#exact-factorization-and-inflation-boundary) · [Binomial dossier](../../../univariateML-cpp/include/cppunivariate/distributions/binom/README.md) · [NB positive conditioning](2026-11-27-trunc-expfam-math-report.md#nbinom-free-zerotruncated)

With inflation held the zero mass is `pi + (1-pi)*f(0)`, and the mass of each positive count is `(1-pi)*f(k)`. Thus positive data require `pi<1`. If there are no zeros, the multiplier on the parent likelihood is constant. If there are only zeros, the fit maximizes the total zero mass, with a flat slice at `pi=1`. At free inflation the clipped optimum joins the ordinary branch exactly at `f(0)=n0/n` with `pi=0`. For example, on `(0,0,1,3)` the geometric conditional mean is two, `prob=1/2` and `f(0)=1/2`, so both expressions give the same fit. The held-inflation comparisons use the actual fixed zero mass throughout. [Inflation factorization](2026-11-27-trunc-expfam-math-report.md#exact-factorization-and-inflation-boundary)

For positive-truncated binomial, a nonconstant sample has its mean in the interior of every feasible positive support, so each finite design has one matched probability. The signed factorial gap and the validated moment envelope control its Poisson tail. At `L=1` the rational positive sample mean implies that an algebraic matched positive rate would make its exponential algebraic, contradicting Hermite–Lindemann. This rules out a zero factorial gap, since the positive-Poisson second factorial moment is the matched mean times the matched rate. It also rules out finite-to-face likelihood equality via the algebraic finite binomial likelihood and the nonconstant polynomial expression for the positive-Poisson likelihood. For modified NB the expansion in inverse size instead gives the positive-Poisson gap with the opposite sign, and analytic continuation at zero and infinity gives a finite stationary list without assuming unimodality. The matched profile covers every finite size and bounds every sequence of likelihoods at that size. With size bounded, probability approaching zero loses all fixed positive masses, even at size zero, since the divided positive normalizer is bounded below by `-log(prob)`. The probability-one atom is admitted. A finite NB candidate meeting the face attains, and the face also reaches the supremum if no candidate is higher. [Binomial face proof](2026-11-25-tbinom-math-report.md#poisson-face-comparison-and-equality) · [Moment envelope](2026-11-25-tbinom2-math-report.md#the-moment-envelope-for-pure-zero-truncation) · [Hermite–Lindemann](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.14352) · [NB profile comparison](2026-11-27-trunc-expfam-math-report.md#nbinom-free-zerotruncated)

In the free-size held-inflation comparisons, a positive observation forces the rescaled parent rate along a positive-height unbounded-size sequence to stay bounded above and away from zero. Each convergent subsequence gives a Poisson parent with the same inflation, and such limits are reachable. For NB, size tending to zero kills every fixed positive mass uniformly in probability as the rising factorial includes a size factor. At bounded positive size either probability end also kills every fixed positive mass. For binomial, both probability endpoints at fixed size are proper and included. These facts allow a direct attainment test against the best same-inflation ZIP face. If some finite candidate reaches the face and no candidate exceeds it, that candidate attains the supremum. If a finite witness exceeds the face, compactness of a superlevel above the face gives attainment. If every finite candidate is strictly below the face, the supremum is unattained, even when the candidate values converge to it. [Binomial face proof](2026-11-27-trunc-expfam-math-report.md#binom-free-zeroinflated) · [NB face proof](2026-11-27-trunc-expfam-math-report.md#nbinom-free-zeroinflated)
