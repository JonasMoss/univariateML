# Existence atlas

A fit regime fixes the held coefficients and the fitting domain, including endpoint conventions and restrictions. Fix the sample and take the limsup of its **joint** log likelihood along feasible parameter vectors approaching a point in a compactification. This gives the sample likelihood envelope. Taking a separate limsup at each observation may combine incompatible paths.

On a compact metrizable space with a dense feasible set, the upper semicontinuous envelope attains its maximum. Its maximum equals the original supremum. Upper semicontinuity of the original likelihood on the feasible set makes the two agree there. When only boundary points maximize the envelope, every maximizing sequence has a subsequence approaching one of them. A proof may instead confine the relevant sequences to a compact region. Identification with a named model also requires a compatible path and equality of its joint likelihood limit with the face value. [Likelihood closure lemma](../ml-behaviour.tex#lem:closure)

The supremum can be attained, approached at a named limit, infinite along a collapse path, or finite and unattained without a named model. Attainment leaves a separate cardinality question. On a singleton feasible domain the supplied parameter vector is the unique maximizer because there are no other feasible points.

The table is generated by `python3 dev/tools/existence_atlas.py --overview`. A one-sided theorem settles only the cases in its hypotheses. For individual paths and detectors see the structured `limits` column. The overview groups by the coverage of the audited proof, so an open cell can contain a proved special case.
